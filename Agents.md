## Privacy and Information an Assistant Must Never See

From my experience working with teams and athletes, privacy and trust are important. I should never paste confidential or private information about teammates, athletes, coaches, staff, or organizations into an AI assistant.

This includes:
- Private team communications or internal team information
- Medical or injury information belonging to another person
- Academic records or other private student information
- Passwords, login credentials, or security information
- Personal identification numbers or financial account information
- Private contact information unless it is necessary and appropriate to share
- Confidential documents or information I do not have permission to disclose

When in doubt, remove identifying or confidential information before using an AI assistant.
